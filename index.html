Proposed extension to Figma Forum Ambassadors

Ambassador Assist

Helping Forum Ambassadors spend their time on the unanswered questions they're best placed to answer, then turning their best answers into guides.

Ambassador view what ambassadors see Community team view internal

Your weekly digest

Pick the areas you like helping with. You'll get up to five open questions a week. Ignore it whenever you're busy.

    How questions are picked

    1. Only how-to and workflow questions from Ask the Community
    2. Questions nobody has answered come first, then the ones more people have viewed
    3. Billing, account access, plugin reviews and bugs stay with Figma Support, so you won't see them

    Your answers, reused

    When an answer is marked as the solution and shows a workflow other people will need, the community team may ask to turn it into a short guide credited to you.

      Four-week pilot with the founding Ambassadors

      1. Week 1Learn their expertiseAsk the six Ambassadors which products and topics they like helping with.Output: an area list for each Ambassador
      2. Week 2Send a curated digestPick up to five matching questions each and review them by hand. The digest is optional.Measure: open rate, questions taken
      3. Week 3Capture reusable answersTurn one or two accepted answers into short guides, with the author's permission.Measure: guides drafted, author approval
      4. Week 4EvaluateCompare against the baseline on this page and ask Ambassadors what to change.Measure: time to first reply, community share of solutions, feedback

      How this was measured

      • All 415 topics in the forum's “Help others” view and the 275 most recently published Ask the Community topics, read on 11 Nov 2026.
      • 120 of those topics had an accepted answer, given between 18 Aug and 7 Nov 2026. Staff means the forum's Figmate rank. Community covers everyone else, including Figma Ambassadors.
      • Ranks are the ones shown today, so earlier answers by current Ambassadors count as community answers.
      • The 65 open Ask the Community questions were sorted into areas and routes by hand. With Gainsight access, its topic tagging and Q&A analytics could do this automatically.
      • The Ambassador program launched on 26 Oct 2026 with six founding members.
